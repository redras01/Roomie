Room parts

Sizes are typical footprints (width × depth) from IKEA and shop listings, or common sizes. Light radius is an estimate. Check your own item and edit after adding.

Sun patches are geometry only: no clouds, trees or curtains. Lights come from the pieces that give light (set a light radius in Edit).
